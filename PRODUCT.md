# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Session authors and reviewers who need to leave the app and read a conversation as a standalone artifact.
- Non-developer collaborators who need to tell user requests, final AI answers, reasoning, and tool activity apart quickly.

## Product Purpose

- Export one session, including linked subagent sessions, as a local static HTML report for review and sharing.
- Preserve the distinction between human intent and AI process: what was asked, what was concluded, what was thought through, and what tools did.

## Brand Personality

- Quiet, utilitarian, evidence-first.
- Native reading surface with clear typographic hierarchy, not a dashboard and not a chat widget.

## Anti-references

- SaaS card grids, badge spam, gradient headlines, chatbot transcript skins.
- Deep navy terminal aesthetics and generic AI-generated page chrome.

## Design Principles

- Final answers are the default reading surface; reasoning and tool activity are present but visually secondary.
- Filtering should be immediate and understandable without prior product knowledge.
- One accent color. Use neutral surfaces, scale, spacing, and labels for structure.
- Keep export files self-contained and readable from the local file system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls.
- Support keyboard focus, visible focus states, system light/dark themes, and reduced motion.
- Do not rely on color alone to distinguish message roles.
